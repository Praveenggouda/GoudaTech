FAQ

Questions?
We've Got Answers.

Learn more about our website development services, pricing and process.

Ask Us Directly
How much does a website cost?

Our website packages start at ₹3,499. The final price depends on the pages, functionality, integrations and specific requirements of your business.

How long does it take to build a website?

A simple website may take several days, while larger websites or applications can take longer. The timeline depends on the scope and content availability.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. We design responsive websites that adapt to mobile phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can I request changes to my website?

Yes. We discuss your requirements, feedback and revisions throughout the project according to the agreed scope.

Do you integrate WhatsApp into websites?

Yes. We can integrate WhatsApp contact buttons, enquiry links and relevant WhatsApp-based workflows depending on your requirements.

Do I need to pay the full amount upfront?

Projects can be arranged with an advance payment and a remaining payment before final delivery, based on the agreed project terms.
